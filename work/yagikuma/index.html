Yagikuma

Opening the Vietnam market from zero

Tokyo, Japan (remote). First Vietnam market representative.

I was the first Vietnam market representative for a Tokyo company that manufactures and distributes water-fillable traffic barriers. In six months I mapped the market, found the gap and secured 2+ strategic partnerships.

The problem

The company had a product and no presence in Vietnam. It needed to know who the key players in Vietnam were, what they sold, and who would make a good partner.

What I did

1. Map before pitching. I analyzed 50+ companies, from suppliers to distributors, comparing their product portfolios, pricing and contracts. The audit showed who the key players were and where the gap sat.

2. Make the pitch usable in Vietnam. I translated and redesigned all the sales materials: slides, one-pagers and partnership brochures.

3. Run the process between two cultures. I arranged the sales process with prospects and organized discovery meetings between headquarters in Japan and prospects in Vietnam. I reported to headquarters every month. Working between the two sides taught me to listen for what people mean, not only what they say.

What happened

The audit led to 2+ strategic partnerships, which became the foundation for market entry.
