Yogi CPA

Building brands from a mission

Orange, CA (remote). The only marketer.

At Yogi CPA I turned a founder's mission into a slogan, a visual identity and a weekly content system that grew new audience reach 30% a month.

The mission

The founder wanted to move clients past the "financial burden" toward better education and a healthier lifestyle. The brand had to sound professional and trustworthy while carrying that mission.

What I did

1. A slogan that carried the mission. We finalized "Beyond your financial balance." It points past the numbers to the life they are meant to support.

2. A visual identity that matched it. I wrote the brand guidelines around sustainable wealth imagery and a green, orange and white palette, and applied them across every social platform and the website, so the account looked consistent and trustworthy.

3. A weekly system. I managed the content calendar in Airtable and Hootsuite for weekly posts across Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and newsletters, and tracked engagement in Excel to see which topics people preferred. New audience reach grew 30% per month and new viewer traffic grew 10% per month.

The lesson: ask what it is for

When we pivoted to video for the first time, neither of us was sure of the expectations. I delivered exactly what my manager asked for without asking what the videos were for, and the result missed the mark and needed heavy rework.

I gathered her feedback and built a process: goal first, then theme and concept, then design elements, then production. One reel used to take several days of back and forth. After the new process I could produce several reels in one day. I still ask "what is the goal?" before any task.

The same method at UniTutor

For UniTutor the mission was different: a friend helping college students survive finals. I built a brand character with that voice, so every post and event felt like it came from the same friend (see UniTutor's case).
