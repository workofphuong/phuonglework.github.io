UniTutor

Launching a new category with free events

Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Led a 5-member cross-functional team.

UniTutor was one of the first tutoring services for college students, and free events turned it into a name people knew, with 10K+ reach per campaign and 150+ enrollments per course.

The problem

A new category has nothing to compare itself with. Students did not yet know what a peer tutoring service was, and they had no reason to trust one. Effort had to go to both demand generation and keeping the customers we won.

What I did

1. Prove the tutors before selling the course. We hosted free career-navigation events as self-development, to show that the tutors were young, talented and able to help beyond academic subjects. Promotion featured the tutors' real profiles.

2. Give the brand a character. I developed a voice that felt like a friend helping college students survive finals, and kept it consistent across LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram.

3. Run it as one system. I led a 5-member team through full-cycle event operations for 100+ attendees, covering themes, speaker partnerships and sponsor coordination. I built cross-department plans in Google Suite so timelines and resources lined up across 5+ course campaigns and seasonal events.

What happened

Event promotion generated 10K reach in a week (318 likes and 74 comments), and the flagship event drew 400+ registrations.

In the next course promotion, enrollment doubled. Across campaigns the results were 10K+ reach per campaign and 150+ enrollments per course. The 5+ course campaigns and seasonal events I planned achieved an 80% participant conversion rate.

Feedback satisfaction stayed above 4 out of 5, which is evidence the product fit the market.
